Building the heart. Our first version was a procedural heart, which is still kept as a fallback. For realism, we moved to the Realistic Human Heart by neshallads on Sketchfab, under CC BY 4.0. A headless Blender script aligns it, scales it to twelve centimetres, and splits it into parts.
